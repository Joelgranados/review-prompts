# Fishers Scuttlebutt Strategy

## Growth Product/Service

Does the company have a product{,line}/service that has a non-saturated large
(enough) addressable market that enables significant sales growth for at least
3-5 years?

Measure:
  * Identify a product(s) or service to analyze
  * Identify the addressable market
  * Identify current sales from company and competitors

## Management determination

Does management proactively develop the next generation of products before
current ones mature?

Measure:
  * R&D investment investment
  * R&D investment in the product{,line}/service

## R&D

Is the R&D transformed into revenue?

Measure:
  * Number of new products/services per period in the past
  * R&D investment investment
  * Number of patents held
  * Number of highly educated employees

## Sales organization

Is the company effective at converting its products into sales

Measure:
  * identify the sales measure (number of customers, retention, number of
    members)

## Profit margin

Does the company have a worth while profit margin?

Measure:
  * Operating Profit Margin. Where all the operating costs are taken into
    account

## Profit Margin Improvement

What is the companies strategy to improve profit margins?

Measure:
  * Identify strategies that are focused on increasing marginal value.

## Labor relations

How well does the company treat employees? Labor and personnel relations:

Measure:
  * Employee turnover rate
  * Number of layoffs in a specific time period
  * Glass door review

## Exec relations

Does the company have outstanding executive relations?

Measure:
  * Time in office (tenure) of CEO, CFO, COO, CTO...

## Competition

What are the industry specific competitive advantages

Measure
  * Identify an industry specific KPI (sales per square foot, number of new
    members per month)
